      Students buy courses with a balance in their account. The balance is topped up in two ways, which keeps the platform usable in markets where online payment is hard.

      Online payment

      1. The admin creates top-up packs. Students see a pack only while it is available.
      2. The student pays by card through Stripe.
      3. The balance is credited automatically.

      Prepaid cards

      1. The admin generates a batch by setting the card value, a suffix and the quantity. Each card gets a unique code.
      2. Cards are sold through the admin or approved points of sale.
      3. The student enters the code and the system checks that it is valid and unused.
      4. The balance is credited immediately.
      Balance in Syrian pounds or US dollars The admin sets the default currency. The balance buys courses, every top-up and deduction is recorded, and none of them can be edited or deleted.
